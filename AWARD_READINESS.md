### **Impact Statement**

**Title:** Agri-Shokti Bangladesh - Empowering Farmers with NASA Technology

**Problem:**
Bangladesh has 16 million farmers facing significant challenges: 40% crop losses, limited access to expert advice, high input costs, and climate uncertainty.

**Solution:**
Agri-Shokti is a free, offline-first Progressive Web App that brings NASA satellite technology, AI-powered disease detection, and community collaboration directly to farmers' smartphones.

**Innovation:**
- First agricultural platform in Bangladesh with real-time NASA GIBS satellite integration
- Free AI disease detection using advanced image recognition
- Unique barter system reducing farmer costs
- Offline-capable with 7-day data caching
- Fully Bengali-language interface

**Impact:**
- **Access:** Works offline on basic smartphones
- **Cost:** Completely free for all farmers
- **Efficiency:** 80-95% faster data loading
- **Sustainability:** Optimized pesticide use, water conservation
- **Community:** Farmer-to-farmer knowledge sharing

**Scalability:**
Serverless architecture supports unlimited farmers. Replicable across developing nations with agricultural economies.

**Future:**
- Voice input for low-literacy farmers
- ML-based yield predictions
- Blockchain crop insurance
- Government integration
