# AI Core: Technical Implementation & Logic Details

This document provides a deep-dive into the "AI Logic Core"—the primary intelligence engine of **Agri Shokti**.

---

## 1. AI Reasoning Engine (LLM Selection)
We use **Gemini 2.5 Flash** as our central Reasoning Engine. 
*   **Why Gemini?**: Its 1M+ token context window and native multimodal (vision/audio/video) processing allow for complex cross-referencing between satellite imagery and text-based PDFs.
*   **Adaptive Reasoning**: The model is prompted to follow a "Chain-of-Thought" (CoT) pattern, ensuring it breaks down agricultural problems into multi-step diagnostic paths.

---

## 2. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Stack
Our solution is "10X better" because it is grounded in fact, not just general knowledge.

*   **Knowledge Retrieval**: We index over 10,000 pages of specialized datasets:
    *   **BARI (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute)** crop manuals.
    *   **DAE (Department of Agricultural Extension)** pest journals.
    *   **SoilGrids/NASA** environmental datasets.
*   **Vector Database**: High-dimension embeddings (generated by `text-embedding-004`) are stored in **Supabase PG Vector**.
*   **Semantic Search**: When a farmer asks a question, we perform a cosine-similarity search to pull the top 5 most relevant context chunks to ground the AI's response.

---

## 3. Agentic Orchestration & MCP Specialists
Agri Shokti uses a multi-agent system where different "specialists" coordinate care.

*   **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: We follow an MCP-style architecture where the central LLM acts as an orchestrator, calling specialized Edge Function "Tools" as agents:
    *   **Vision Specialist**: Detects leaf diseases and reads fertilizer bag OCR.
    *   **Market Specialist**: Analyzes live price streams to identify favorable selling windows.
    *   **Geospatial Specialist**: Processes NASA/Sentinel-2 imagery to generate NDVI health scores.
*   **Workflow Automation**: **Supabase Edge Functions** handle the transition from "AI Reasoning" to "Platform Action," such as sending alerts or updating the pest heatmap.

---

## 4. Data Strategy & Decision Logic
We ensure "AI Explainability" through a structured ingestion and verification pipeline.

*   **Preprocessing Pipeline**: Raw voice/text/image data is normalized and summarized before being fed into the LLM core to optimize battery and API efficiency.
*   **Decision Paths**: The AI follows a "Verification Pass" where it compares its generated advice against the retrieved RAG context.
*   **Hallucination Check**: If the generated advice conflicts significantly with the BARI knowledge manual, the system triggers a **hallucination flag** and redirects the user to a verified PDF source.

---

## 5. Domain-Specific Models
*   **Computer Vision**: Custom vision-prompting in Gemini handles agricultural-specific visual features (e.g., leaf rust patterns, NPK branding inconsistencies on fertilizer sacks).
*   **Geospatial Intelligence**: Our system calculates the **Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)** by processing Near-Infrared (NIR) and Red spectral bands, which is then interpreted by the LLM to explain "Crop Stress" in plain Bengali.
